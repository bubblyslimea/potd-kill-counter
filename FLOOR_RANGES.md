# potD passage kill ranges

these apply to ordinary floors
the hidden requirement is randomized within a floor set; the tracker reports a range, not an exact target
values were checked against Deep Dungeon Compendium on 2026-11-02

Flight used on one floor applies to the **next** floor
it halves the requirement, rounded up
clearing all enemies can unlock the passage earlier than the listed minimum
the actual game passage state always wins

floors 10, 20, …, 190 are boss floors; they do not use the ordinary kill quota
floor 200 is the completion floor

the wider estimates retain rare 3/9 outcomes on 41–49 and 91–99, and rare 10 outcomes on 101–149. These are observed community ranges, not a guarantee that every floor must hit those exact limits

example: on floor 193, after four observed kills and with passage still closed, the estimate is **1–9 more** normally or **1–3 more** with Flight active
with an incomplete count or unknown Flight, the plugin shows a wider, qualified remaining range
partial counts start at one, unknown Flight covers both possible targets
